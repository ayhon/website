Hello! My name is Fernando Leal, known in the internet as `ayhon`!

I am a computer scientist interested in programming languages, software verification ant theorem provers.
In general, I am likely to enjoy any topic in the intersection of Computer Science and Mathematics.

In 2024 I finished my Computer Science and Mathematics Double Bachelor at [UCM] in Madrid, Spain.
That same year I started my masters in Research in Computer Science at [ENS Paris-Saclay].

During my first year of my masters, I had an internship in Inria's [Prosecco] team under the supervision of
[Aymeric Fromherz] and [Son Ho], where I worked with [Aeneas] to verify a [SHA3 implementation](https://github.com/ayhon/sha3.rs) in Lean.
Some additional autocomes of this internship were the initial `step*` tactic implementation for Aeneas and
an executable Lean [SHA3 specification](https://github.com/ayhon/sha3.lean).

During my second year of my masters, I had an interenship in ETH's [Programming Language Foundations](https://plf.inf.ethz.ch/) lab
under the supervision of [Ralf Jung] and [Max Vistrup], where I worked on the [Iris-Lean] project by
porting the programming language interface and developing [Wander], a Lean native Iris automation tactic.

For the end of 2026, I will collaborate with [Jonathan Protzenko]'s team as part of the [Google Student Researcher Program],
where I will contribute to existing projects ([Kraken], [Aeneas]) as a Lean metaprogrammer.
